# Elmfield Community Hospital - Visiting Times Policy

Policy ECH-PS-101. Effective 2026-01-01.

## General inpatient wards
Visitors are welcome daily from 10:00 am to 8:00 pm. Two visitors may
be at the bedside at a time; others may wait in the family room on
each floor. Ward staff may ask visitors to step out during protected
mealtimes (12:00 pm to 1:00 pm), except carers who are helping a
patient to eat.

## Intensive Care Unit (ICU)
ICU visiting is 11:00 am to 8:00 pm daily, two visitors at a time,
close family preferred. Please phone the unit on 020 7946 0150 before
travelling, as visits may be paused during clinical procedures.
Children under 12 need approval from the nurse in charge before
visiting the ICU.

## Maternity Unit
Birth partners and support persons are welcome 24 hours a day. Other
visitors, including siblings of the newborn: 10:00 am to 8:00 pm.

## Emergency Department (A&E)
One support person per patient in treatment areas, at the care team's
discretion during procedures.

## Overnight stays
One adult family member may stay overnight with a patient in a side
room with the ward manager's approval. Overnight guests are not
permitted in the ICU.

## Rest hour
Rest hour is 2:00 pm to 3:00 pm daily. Visits are permitted during
rest hour; please keep noise to a minimum.

## Infection control
Please do not visit if you have a cold, flu-like symptoms, diarrhoea
or vomiting, or have had them in the last 48 hours. Use the hand gel
at the entrance to every ward. Assistance dogs are welcome.
