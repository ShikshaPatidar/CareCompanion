# Elmfield Community Hospital - Charges and Payments FAQ

Policy ECH-FIN-204. For anything not covered here, call the Patient
Advice Team on 020 7946 0119 (Mon-Fri, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm).

## Will I be charged for my treatment?
NHS-funded treatment is free at the point of use for people who are
eligible. Some patients who live outside the UK may be asked to pay.
If that applies to you, the Overseas Visitors Team will contact you
before treatment, except in an emergency. Emergency care is never
delayed over payment.

## Can I get a copy of my medical records?
Yes. You can ask for a copy of your records at any time by contacting
the Patient Advice Team. There is no charge, and we aim to respond
within one month.

## Do I have to pay for medicines?
Medicines given to you to take home when you leave hospital do not
need to be paid for. Charges may apply to prescriptions issued for
you to collect from a community pharmacy, unless you are exempt. Ask
the pharmacy team if you are unsure.

## Parking
Parking at the hospital is pay-on-exit: the first 30 minutes are free,
then hourly charges apply. Blue Badge holders may park free of charge
in the marked bays. Payment machines are at each car park entrance.

## Help with travel costs
Some patients on qualifying benefits or low incomes can claim back
travel costs for hospital appointments. Ask the Patient Advice Team
for the claim form.

## I am not happy with my care or a bill. Who do I speak to?
Contact the Patient Advice and Liaison Service (PALS) on
020 7946 0119, or ask any member of staff to put you in touch.
