# Elmfield Community Hospital - Discharge Process

Policy ECH-CL-310.

## The day before discharge
Your care team confirms your going-home plan: medicines to take home,
follow-up appointments, any equipment, and your transport. Ask
questions early - the day of discharge is busy.

## On the day of discharge
1. A doctor writes the discharge order, usually before 11:00 am.
2. Your nurse reviews the discharge summary with you, including your
   medicines and warning signs.
3. Medicines to take home are prepared by the hospital pharmacy in
   Outpatients Block A. This can take a few hours, so you may wait in
   the Discharge Lounge.
4. Patients are escorted to the main entrance; your lift home should
   wait in the 15-minute drop-off zone.

## After you leave
- Your discharge summary is sent to your GP within 48 hours.
- A discharge nurse phones within 2 working days to check how you are.
- Follow-up appointments on your summary are confirmed by text message
  and by letter.

## If you feel worse at home
Call the ward advice line on 020 7946 0142, any time. For urgent
advice that is not an emergency, call NHS 111. For emergencies -
chest pain, trouble breathing, severe bleeding, stroke symptoms -
call 999.
