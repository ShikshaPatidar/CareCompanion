# Elmfield Community Hospital - Outpatient Appointments and Cancellations

Policy ECH-OP-120.

## Before your appointment
Bring your appointment letter or text message, a form of photo ID and
a list of your current medicines. Please arrive 15 minutes early. If
you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to rebook you.

## Blood tests
Blood tests are booked by appointment at Phlebotomy, Outpatients
Block A, Level 1. If your blood test is on the same day as a clinic
appointment, please have the blood test first.

## Changing or cancelling an appointment
Please give at least 48 hours' notice if you cannot attend. Call the
Outpatient Booking Team on 020 7946 0160 (Mon-Fri, 8:00 am to
6:00 pm) or reply CANCEL to the appointment text message. We will
offer you another date.

## If you miss an appointment
If you miss an appointment without letting us know, we will contact
you to offer a new date. If you miss two appointments in a row, the
clinician will review your care with your GP.

## Accessibility and interpreters
Free interpreters, including British Sign Language, are available.
Please ask at least 5 working days before your appointment. Wheelchairs
are available at the main entrance, and hearing loops are fitted at
every reception desk.
